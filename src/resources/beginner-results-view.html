How to read this page

  • Each row is one platform. Base is the old build, New is your change.
  • The Change column shows how much New differs from Base. Lower is faster for this test, so green means the new build got faster.
  • The Verdict tells you whether the difference is real or just measurement noise. Computed from the Mann–Whitney U test under the hood — you don't need to know the math.
  • Click Show distribution to see every individual run plotted as a curve. Two curves that overlap = no real difference. Two curves that pull apart = a real shift.
